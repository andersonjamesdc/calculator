Retirement Planner

A retirement planner for Americans deciding where to retire: staying in the United States, or moving to Canada, France or Spain. It takes a couple’s savings, Social Security, spending and plans, and works out, year by year to age 99, what they can spend, what each country will tax, and how likely the money is to last.

What it is for

  • Comparing where and how to live: start in the US or Canada, France or Spain; rent or buy; move later; keep or sell a home abroad.
  • Seeing what you can spend each year, tested against 1,000 market paths drawn from 1928–2025 history, with stress tests for currency, an early death and long-term care.
  • Choosing when each of you claims Social Security, and printing the plan as a full financial report, a guide to life abroad and a user guide.

What sets it apart

  • Foreign tax, country by country. The US taxes its citizens wherever they live. The planner applies the other country’s tax as well (Spain and Catalonia with the wealth tax, France with its real-estate wealth tax and flat tax, Canada and Québec), then the US foreign tax credit and the US–Spain and US–France treaties, so income is not taxed twice. That is 39 rules in 10 jurisdictions, and every figure shows its source: the treaty article or tax bracket, in a “How this was calculated” note and a line-by-line tax detail for any year.
  • Tax mitigation planning. Optional strategies (a life annuity, a Roth conversion ladder and a brokerage account) each show what they save, and a search finds the best settings for living in Spain, where the income and wealth taxes weigh most.
  • Two currencies. Savings in dollars, life in euros: every figure shows in USD or EUR at an exchange rate you set, and the market simulations move the exchange rate along with returns, so currency risk is part of the odds.

It runs in your web browser or as a phone app, with no account and no server: what you enter stays on your device. Not financial, legal or tax advice.

Open an app, then add it to your home screen (Safari: Share, then Add to Home Screen; Chrome: Install app). It works with no network once installed. On a computer, download the demo as one file.
