Retirement Planner Demo for a computer

The full calculator with a sample household, as one file (Demo 3.16). Download it and open it in your web browser: every feature works on your computer, with no network, including the PDFs, the Excel workbook and the tax detail window.

Download the file (6.2 MB)Download as a .zip (2.5 MB)

Open it

  1. Download the file (or the .zip, and unzip it).
  2. Double-click Retirement_Planner_Demo.html. It opens in your web browser: Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
  3. Try the Look menu and Basic or Advanced; the Financial PDF, Life Abroad PDF and User Guide buttons save PDFs to your Downloads folder.

Good to know

  1. The household, its figures and its home are invented. Change any figure to see your own numbers.
  2. Nothing you type leaves your computer. Scenarios you save stay in that browser on that computer.
  3. The AI Advisor needs your own Anthropic API key; it is the only part that uses the network.
  4. If your browser shows the page instead of saving it, use the .zip, or File, then Save Page As.
